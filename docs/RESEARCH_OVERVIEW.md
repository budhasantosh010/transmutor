# Research Overview

Transmutor began with the question: what computational umbrella could sit above fixed programs and fixed neural architectures?

The current working hypothesis is that intelligence may depend on continually deciding what computation, memory, structure, relationship, primitive, and learning/search process should exist under finite resources.

Across the experiments, a repeated multi-level pattern emerged:

1. create candidate variation;
2. gather evidence;
3. evaluate independently/protected when possible;
4. keep useful candidates and remove harmful ones;
5. reuse successful structures;
6. compress repeated structures into higher-level abstractions;
7. treat those abstractions as new candidates and repeat.

This pattern has appeared at the level of cells, relationships, organs, primitives, primitive libraries, and search strategies.

These are controlled experimental results, not evidence of AGI or a demonstrated Transformer replacement.
