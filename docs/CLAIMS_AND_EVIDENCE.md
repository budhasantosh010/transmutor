# Claims and Evidence

This file is intentionally conservative. It will be expanded from the registry and research logs.

## Strongly supported in controlled experiments

- More capacity alone does not guarantee improvement.
- Structural/relationship credit can matter when components are weak individually but useful together.
- Candidate abstractions benefit from sandboxed or independent proof before deployment.
- Different relationship types can require different evidence channels (e.g. synergy, coverage, temporal direction).
- Causal/search contexts can improve weak-signal discovery, but hard context boundaries can be wrong.
- Learned reusable primitives can greatly reduce future search when their reuse domain matches.
- Additional primitives can also increase branching and hurt unrelated tasks.
- When routing evidence is weak, maintaining protected alternative search strategies can be safer than premature commitment.

## Not established

- AGI.
- A general post-Transformer architecture.
- Superiority to Transformers on realistic matched-compute benchmarks.
- Scaling to very large populations or hardware-efficient implementations.
