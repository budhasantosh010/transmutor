# Post-V836 Frontier Audit

## Scope and evidence standard

This audit covers every registry record from V450 through V836: **428 records spanning all 387 numeric versions V450..V836 with no numeric gaps**. The machine-readable matrix is `experiments/post_v836_frontier.json` and is generated by `scripts/build_frontier_audit.py`.

The archive remains immutable. This audit classifies preserved evidence; it does not fill missing history by guesswork. A scaffold flag of `yes` means the preserved evidence explicitly names that scaffold. `unknown` means the migrated evidence is insufficient to prove presence or absence. It does **not** mean the scaffold was absent.

Evidence preservation is uneven across the range. Rich narrative logs survive for V744–V784 and V801–V836; many earlier V450–V743 records survive only as figures, result JSONs, or registry entries. The matrix therefore records an `evidence_level` for every row.

## What V450–V836 already established

### Experiment / acquisition control — substantial progress, still partial

V450 explicitly tested long-horizon acquisition **without named DIST/DISAG**. The preserved figure shows the learned long-horizon policy well below the DIST reference on held-out adequate solve rate. V450B listwise ranking also underperformed the reference. V452 generic information gain closed much of the gap, and later V456/V461/V462 learned state-dependent acquisition mixtures, stopping, and value-of-evidence decisions, but those experiments still rely on supplied feature spaces, acquisition families, objectives, or thresholds.

**Conclusion:** active scientific control is not solved at the strongest requested level. The system has learned when/where to gather evidence in several controlled settings, but a general experiment program discovered from raw candidate/world descriptions remains open.

### Neutral substrate differentiation — demonstrated, but not yet primitive invention

V486 shows the same neutral-cell substrate differentiating into different recurrent structure for STATIC_XOR, DELAYED_COPY, and CONDITIONAL_ROUTE. V487 is a fresh replication. V488 demonstrates adequacy-triggered growth of a neutral cell body.

These experiments establish useful differentiation/growth from a common low-level substrate. They do **not** establish the stronger post-V836 primitive-invention criterion: recurrent motif discovery across independent runs, held-out causal value, compression into a callable unit, and later reuse that reduces future search/compute.

**Conclusion:** the neutral substrate exists as a promising base, but reusable primitive invention from it remains open.

### Local structural invention — strong controlled evidence, still scaffolded

V754–V768 evolve/prune local cells, wiring, operators, and learning/death behavior under scalar reward and local causal credit. V759 starts with target cells absent and creates them through birth/mutation. V764B evolves both operator and wiring. V770–V778 add interaction-aware bonds, local organs, irreducible higher-order credit, adaptive interaction order, hierarchical compression, a pure-high-order negative control, and finite-budget abstention. V779–V784 add protected randomized audit probes, candidate-organ competition, structural novelty, adaptive exploration, misleading-evidence controls, and exploration-policy portfolios. V809 later shows sandboxed candidate organs.

The important remaining scaffold is that the edit/operator/action vocabulary itself is still supplied: examples include ADD/SUB/MUL genome families, bond statistics, birth/death/mutation operations, and fixed forms of audit intervention.

**Conclusion:** structural selection and composition are strongly demonstrated in synthetic settings; invention of the restructuring language itself is partial/open.

### Primitive and macro invention — real progress, but the deepest vocabulary is still supplied

V744 discovers a recurrent memory update from sparse final reward. V745B/V746 mine and behaviorally canonicalize reusable update macros. V748 shows zero-shot composed-program transfer. V824B starts from NAND, synthesizes candidate Boolean macros, admits only those with held-out reuse value, and obtains large search reductions. V830B exposes a learned continuous multiplication organ as a recursively callable primitive. V831 automatically discovers call graphs over ADD/SUB/frozen learned MUL. V834 automatically promotes a repeated second-generation structure.

However the surviving V813–V827 research log explicitly names two open problems:

- discover primitive signatures from lower-level continuous cells, not symbolic truth tables;
- integrate macro invention with evolving cells/organs/memory rather than a separate symbolic searcher.

V764B's "operator evolution" still selects from a human-defined operator genome. V824B starts from NAND. V831 receives ADD, SUB, and a frozen learned MUL primitive. V834 mines patterns over an already named symbolic vocabulary.

**Conclusion:** macro invention and reuse are solved in important controlled subcases; **human-defined primitive/operator vocabulary remains the deepest unresolved scaffold**.

### Search control — portfolios/admission are strong, search-program invention is not

V826 shows early primitive-library routing is unreliable. V827 shows a protected BASE/MACRO portfolio gives a hard 2x regret bound. V828B shows adaptive strategy weighting can catastrophically underfund rare strategies. V828C maps the exploration-floor tradeoff. V833B/V834B protect old/new vocabularies in parallel. V835 demonstrates portfolio explosion. V836 then selects compact complete libraries by held-out development coverage/regret and is historically recorded PASS.

These are strong results about allocating among supplied strategies. The actual search procedures remain named procedures/portfolios rather than learned program/state-machine objects that invent `propose/evaluate/retain/restart/refine/branch/stop` behavior.

**Conclusion:** search-strategy allocation is substantially solved; search-strategy **invention** remains partial/open.

### Recursive abstraction — demonstrated in pieces, not yet unified

The archive contains recursive macro mining, automatic call-graph discovery, hierarchical interaction compression, second-generation macro mining, and library-level admission. This is evidence that abstraction can recurse across several levels. It is not yet one common archive in which task programs, primitives, restructuring programs, experiment programs, and search programs share one compositional interface and recursively call/promote each other.

**Conclusion:** recursive abstraction is partial; a unified recursive abstraction substrate should follow—not precede—the removal of the deeper primitive-vocabulary scaffold.

## Frontier decision

**Deepest remaining human-supplied scaffold:** the high-level primitive/operator vocabulary that sits underneath later macro, search, and abstraction machinery.

**Next scientific target if the V836 recovery gate becomes unblocked:** Target A, specifically **callable primitive invention from the existing neutral-cell substrate**, not another differentiation-only neutral-substrate experiment.

The experiment must test all four primitive-invention requirements together:

1. a repeated motif appears independently;
2. the motif improves held-out performance;
3. the motif can be compressed into a callable reusable unit;
4. reuse improves later search/compute under a fixed budget.

It must use the same low-level substrate across behaviorally different families with no task-family label and must compare against no-promotion, random promotion, frequency-only promotion, fixed architecture, and an oracle hand-designed motif where meaningful.

## Why no V837 is executed in this branch

The new recovery rules require historical V836 reproduction before subsequent science is treated as valid continuation. The migrated registry references the original `transmutor_v828_v836_experiments.zip`, but that exact source archive is absent from the repository and migration package. The V836 reproduction harness therefore correctly returns `CANNOT_REPRODUCE_MISSING_SOURCE`.

Historical V836 stays **PASS**. It is not rewritten. But Gate B of the new completion standard is blocked, the exact historical pass-gate implementation and seed policy are not preserved, and no separate pristine fresh audit is preserved. Starting V837 anyway would directly violate the user's frontier program. The next experiment is therefore specified and frozen as a proposal only; it is **not run**.
